# Model card

## Models

RandomForest, HistGradientBoosting, LogisticRegression and an optional 1D-CNN over the 64x3 sequence tensor.

## Intended use

Research and education on encrypted-traffic metadata. Not for production detection or individual profiling.

## Training data

Synthetic flows generated by `cipherscope.datasets.synthetic`, plus optional registered research datasets loaded with explicit licence acceptance.

## Evaluation

Grouped splits by host, time, capture, dataset and environment; macro-F1, PR-AUC, FPR at 90% recall; bootstrap confidence intervals over at least three seeds; calibration ECE before and after; robustness under padding, jitter and truncation.

## Limitations

Synthetic data is labelled as such. Metrics show the pipeline works; they are not evidence of real-world detection. Beacon detection has documented false positives (NTP, polling apps, load balancers).
